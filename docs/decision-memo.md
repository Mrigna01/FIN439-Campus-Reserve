# Campus Scholarship Reserve decision memo

I used ChatGPT through Codex to adapt the app, check calculations and help prepare this memo. I am responsible for the analysis and committee decision. This is for education, not investment advice.

**Approve a paper pilot of 75% SGOV and 25% VT. Hold funding.** Request that the treasurer verify forward return assumptions, client costs, and the operational mandate before bringing any trade back for approval.

## Mandate and alternatives

The fictional $1 million reserve has a seven-year horizon and pays $10,000 quarterly. It seeks CPI + 3% annually net over rolling five-year periods. The case uses an illustrative 2.50% inflation input and a 5.50% modeled hurdle. The reserve must hold at least 4% initially in eligible government securities or cash, recalculated against the actual portfolio value at review. Investments are long-only, fully invested, and unlevered within the four-ETF universe. Other rules are 90% available within five business days, an 8% aggregate corporate issuer cap subject to the cash/Treasury/diversified-fund exemptions, annual rebalance, and committee approval above 25% annual turnover. Estimated annual volatility is the selection measure.

|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Equal weight | 25 / 25 / 25 / 25% | 3.76% | 6.51% | 50% | Misses return hurdle |

| Constrained | 75 / 0 / 0 / 25% | 5.66% | 3.82% | 75% | Passes modeled rules |

The benchmark carries forward the Lab 14 policy comparison of diversified global equity and investment-grade corporate bonds. Its fixed weights give the committee an interpretable risk comparison without relying on fitted estimates. It has no dedicated Treasury buffer. The constrained mix was selected from 1,771 fully invested five-point grid allocations, of which 392 passed the modeled rules. Liquidity, issuer exposure, turnover and dated payouts remain open.

## Limits and review triggers

The November 17, 2026 decision uses 60 monthly returns ending September 30, 2026, from the October 22 archived snapshot. The selected mix estimates 5.76% before additional costs and 5.66% after an illustrative 10 bp annual client cost. Fund expenses are embedded in historical prices. The 0.16-point modeled net cushion is narrow. Neither the arithmetic mean nor the VT 16.45% trailing signal is a forward return forecast. A nonpositive or unavailable signal puts the paper pilot on review.

The one-at-a-time cases that still have a feasible solution span **70–90% SGOV and 10–30% VT**. Use that range to monitor sensitivity, with the base mix as the paper starting point. The latest 36 months move the optimizer 15 points toward SGOV. A one-point cut to VT's estimate takes the original mix to 5.41% net; 30 bp cost separately takes it to 5.46%. Reoptimization in either case raises VT to 30%, which needs review and fresh approval. The 80% reserve stress leaves no qualifying grid allocation. Stop or review when the existing mix falls below the 5.50% net hurdle, breaches the applicable reserve floor, or fails any verified execution check. Revisit the inflation assumption, actual expenses, reserve value and payout dates at each committee review.
